A LITTLE TEA RITUAL

Make a moment of it.

For hibiscus tea, add 1–2 teaspoons to a cup of freshly boiled water. Steep for 5–10 minutes to your taste, then strain. Try it hot or chilled, with lemon, ginger or a little honey.
